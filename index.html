Everything you need to get ORTH working for your farm. Start with your plots. End with intelligence that moves with the season.

Time to first plotUnder 5 minutes
ORTH on desktop.

ORTH is available on web as well as mobile. The desktop experience gives you a fuller view of your plots, categories, and agronomic intelligence across a wider canvas.

Everything stays in sync. What you set up on mobile is immediately available on web, and vice versa.

Step 1
Download the app
and sign up.

ORTH is available on iOS, Android, and web. Search for ORTH in the App Store or Google Play, or open app.orth.com in any browser.

You'll need an email address and a farm name to get started. If your operation has multiple users, you can add the rest of your team once your account is set up.

Available on
App Store, Google Play & Web
Step 2
Create
your farm.

Before you add plots, ORTH asks you to set up your farm. Give it a name and location. This becomes the top-level container for everything ORTH tracks on your behalf.

If you manage more than one farm or operation, you can create multiple farms and switch between them at any time.

Step 3
Draw your
plots on the map.

ORTH builds its intelligence around your land. Use the map view to draw the boundaries of each plot. Tap Draw, trace the perimeter, and confirm.

You can draw as many plots as your operation requires. Each one becomes an independent unit that ORTH tracks separately.

Trace plot boundaries as precisely as you can. ORTH uses the shape and area to calculate irrigation volumes, spray coverage, and yield projections.

Step 4
Name
your plot.

Give each plot a name you'll recognize in the field. Most growers use a combination of block position and variety, like Lote Norte or Lote Sur.

The name stays with every insight, alert, and recommendation ORTH sends you. Make it specific enough to act on immediately.

Step 5
Select
your crop.

Tell ORTH what you're growing. Choose your crop from the list and then select the variety. ORTH's agronomic models are built around specific crop-variety combinations, so the more accurate this is, the more relevant everything that follows will be.

If your variety isn't listed, choose the closest match and let your agronomist know. ORTH's crop library is updated regularly.

Step 6
Confirm
your variety.

Within your crop, select the specific variety growing in this plot. Variety determines the growth model ORTH applies, including sensitivity thresholds, phenological timing, and water demand curves.

If a plot carries more than one variety, create a separate plot for each.

Step 7
Fill in your
plot details.

ORTH asks for a handful of agronomic details about each plot: soil type, irrigation system, planting density, and plantation date.

These are not administrative fields. Every answer shapes the models ORTH runs for your land. Fill in what you know. You can update anything later.

Step 8
Upload your
records.

If you have previous harvest data, soil analyses, or lab reports, upload them here. ORTH reads these documents and uses them to build a starting picture of your land that would otherwise take a full season to accumulate.

Any format works. Photos of paper records included.

This step is optional but it matters. Growers who upload historical records see more accurate recommendations from the first week of use.

Step 9
Choose
where to start.

Once your plots are set up, ORTH offers you a set of starting prompts based on your crop and current conditions. These are specific to what is happening on your land right now.

Pick one to begin, or write your own question directly.

Step 10
Ask your
first question.

ORTH is ready to answer questions about your plots from the moment setup is complete. Ask anything you'd ask an experienced agronomist: about your irrigation schedule, a leaf you're unsure about, what the next ten days mean for your crop.

ORTH answers in the context of your specific plots, your crop, and your current conditions.

Step 11
Check your
daily insights.

Every morning, ORTH surfaces what matters most for that day across your plots. Irrigation timing, pest pressure signals, weather-driven risk. You don't have to go looking for it.

Each insight links directly to a recommendation you can act on. The objective is to start the day knowing what it asks of you.

Step 12
Open the
Weather Center.

The Weather Center gives you a hyperlocal forecast for your exact plots, not the nearest town. Temperature, wind, humidity, and ETo are tracked and interpreted in terms of what they mean for your crop at this point in the season.

Weather in ORTH is not a widget. It is a variable that runs through every recommendation the system makes.

Step 13
Review
your alerts.

Alerts are ORTH's signal that something requires your attention today. Heat stress approaching a threshold. A pest pressure building in Lote Este. A window to act before the forecast closes it.

Alerts are ranked by urgency and tied to the specific plot they concern. Tap any alert to see the full picture and the recommended response.
